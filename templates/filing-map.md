# Filing map
_Where Ariadne files things. This is a starting point: `/tidy setup` tailors it to you, and you can edit it any
time. Rename, renumber or add areas freely; Ariadne proposes a rename batch to match._

Root: `~/Documents` (in iCloud if Desktop & Documents sync is on)

## Areas
Numbers keep the order fixed.

| Area | Subfolders |
|---|---|
| 00 Inbox | `_Review` (unsure), `_Duplicates` (copies for you to delete) |
| 10 Home | 11 Vital Records · 12 House · 13 Vehicles · 14 Health · 15 Family · 16 Pets |
| 20 Finance | 21 Banking · 22 Investments & Retirement · 23 Taxes/YYYY · 24 Insurance · 25 Debts · 26 Budget |
| 30 Legal | 31 Estate Plan · 32 Contracts & Disputes |
| 40 Businesses | one folder per business (41, 42, …), each with the business template below |
| 50 Career | 51 <current employer> · 52 Resume & CV · 53 Education & Certifications · 59 Past Employers |
| 60 Projects & Interests | one numbered folder per project or hobby |
| 70 Reference | articles, manuals, fonts, saved research |
| 90 Archive | closed years, closed businesses, old exports, installers |

**Business template:** 01 Formation & Compliance · 02 Banking & Books · 03 Taxes · 04 Contracts & Clients · 05 <its own work> ·
06 Brand Assets. A business's records live inside it, even tax and legal ones. When one business holds another's shared assets,
the others get an alias to them (`ariadne alias`), never a copy.

## Naming
- Dated files: `YYYY-MM-DD Source Description.ext` (the document's own date, not the download date).
- Statements: `YYYY-MM Source Statement.ext`. Year folders: `YYYY`.
- No "final", "v2" or "copy" in names; superseded versions go to 90 Archive.

## Notes
Notes folders mirror the areas above (10 Home, 20 Finance, …). The default Notes folder is the Notes inbox. A note that is really a
record (a statement, a contract summary, a finished write-up) becomes a file in its area, and the note moves to 90 Archive.

## Photos and videos
Photos: Family · Events & Trips · By Year · Imports & Backups · Archive. Videos: Home Videos · Unsorted. Content footage for a
business goes to that business's media folder. Camera media nobody has sorted waits in `_Camera Media` and is never uploaded by surprise.

## Never touch
App-owned folders (Adobe, Zoom, Photos and Lightroom libraries, and others), code folders and git repositories, and anything you add with
`ariadne protect`.
